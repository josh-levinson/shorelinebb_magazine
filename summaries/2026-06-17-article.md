# Shoreline Tips Off With Three Nail-Biters and a Glass-Cleaning Masterclass

The new season opened Wednesday night at East Shoreline Catholic Academy, and the basketball gods skipped the easing-in period entirely: two three-point thrillers, a 22-rebound monster game, and the Huskies planting an early flag atop the table.

## Around the League

**Red Storm 43, Knicks 40.** The night's tightest finish was a rock fight to the very end. **David Orr** set the tone for Red Storm with a game-high 16 points to go with 5 rebounds, a steal, a block and a triple, while **Jason Erxleben** did the dirty work — a 10-point, 11-rebound double-double with 2 assists. **Branden LaPia** chipped in 8 points and 5 boards, and **Danny Gardner** grabbed 8 rebounds with a pair of steals. The Knicks had every reason to walk away frustrated: **Taylor Houghton** was sensational with 17 points, 10 rebounds and 2 blocks, and **Brian Jackson** filled the sheet with 11 points, 8 rebounds, 4 assists and 2 steals. **Scott Edmond** added 6 points and 8 boards, but the three-point gap held.

**Bulldogs 44, Black Mamba 41.** Another three-point squeaker, and another night where one hot hand made the difference. **Khalid Jabarkhail** poured in 18 points on the strength of 4 made threes, adding 5 rebounds and 2 steals. **Dan Scandalito** backed him with a 10-point, 8-rebound, 2-steal effort that included 2 triples of his own. Black Mamba pushed right to the wire behind **Paul Votto**'s 11-point, 11-rebound double-double (2 steals to boot), with **Colby Morawski** (8 points, 7 rebounds, 4 assists) and **Samim Jabarkhail** (7 points, 7 rebounds, a game-high 3 steals) doing a little of everything. **Justin Rozen**'s 6 points and 8 boards rounded out a balanced effort that came up just short.

**Huskies 57, Green Wave 48.** The lone game with breathing room still had plenty of fireworks. **Ryan Florentine** led all scorers with 23 points, stuffing the line with 6 rebounds, 3 assists, 2 steals and a block. **Scott Houghton** caught fire from deep, knocking down 3 of his triples on the way to 15 points. But the headline belonged to **Neil Anglemire**, who hauled in a staggering 22 rebounds alongside 8 points and 3 assists. Green Wave didn't go quietly — **Jeremy McDonald** stuffed the box score with 18 points, 7 rebounds, 5 assists and 3 steals, and **Jimmy Girolamo** added 16 points on 3 made threes — but the Huskies' two-way punch carried the night.

## The Standings

With everyone playing exactly once, the early table is a study in tiebreakers. The **Huskies** sit atop the league on the strength of the week's biggest margin (+9), with the **Bulldogs** and **Red Storm** right behind them — both 1-0 with identical +3 differentials. On the other side of those three-point games, **Black Mamba** and the **Knicks** find themselves at 0-1 despite competitive showings, while **Green Wave** rounds out the field. It's Week 1, so nobody's panicking — there's a long way to go.

## Movers & Shakers

**Bucket of the Week** goes to **Ryan Florentine**, whose 23 points led every scorer in the league — and he did it without taking a night off on defense (2 steals, a block). Right behind him, a three-way scoring chase: **Jeremy McDonald** and **Khalid Jabarkhail** each dropped 18, with Khalid's coming on a league-best 4 triples. **Taylor Houghton** (17) and **David Orr** (16) weren't far off.

On the glass, nobody came close to **Neil Anglemire**, whose 22 rebounds were the number of the night. **McDonald** topped the assist column with 5 dimes, **Samim Jabarkhail** swiped a league-high 3 steals, and **Houghton** paced the rim protectors with 2 blocks.

And the **double-double club** opened its season with three charter members: **Taylor Houghton** (17 & 10), **Paul Votto** (11 & 11) and **Jason Erxleben** (10 & 11). A strong opening night for the do-it-all crowd.
