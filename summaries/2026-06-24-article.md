# Red Storm Weather a Two-Point Thriller to Seize the Top Spot

A single possession separated first from a very different Wednesday night at East Shoreline Catholic Academy, where the Red Storm outlasted the Bulldogs 46-44 and two forfeits reshuffled the middle of the table.

## Around the League

The marquee — and, frankly, only — game with a scoreboard worth arguing over was **Red Storm 46, Bulldogs 44**, a two-point classic decided in the margins. **David Orr** was the biggest man in the building, stuffing the box score with 25 points, 12 rebounds, 2 assists, a steal and 2 blocks — a two-way clinic that carried Red Storm across the line. He had plenty of help from **Jason Erxleben**, who quietly put together a 12-point, 10-rebound double-double with 2 assists, a steal and a block. Role players did role-player things: **James Holt** knocked down a triple in his 3-point night, and **Doug Brackney** chipped in a bucket.

The Bulldogs had every reason to feel good despite the loss, mostly because **Khalid Jabarkhail** turned into a flamethrower. His 20 points came on a jaw-dropping six made threes, and he rounded out the line with 6 rebounds, 2 assists, a steal and a block. **Joe Flaherty** added 8, **Dan Scandalito** filled the stat sheet with 5 points, 6 boards, 3 assists and a triple of his own, and **Mason Carey** did the dirty work — 4 points, 7 rebounds, 3 assists and 2 blocks. Two points short, but there's no shame in this one.

The other two games on the slate never got off the ground. The **Knicks** took a 2-0 win over the **Huskies** by forfeit, and the **Green Wave** picked up a 2-0 forfeit win over **Black Mamba**. No players suited up on the losing sides in either, so the scores are just the league's bookkeeping convention — no highlights to relive here, just a couple of Ws logged without a ball bouncing.

## The Standings

The forfeits did the heavy lifting on the table this week. **Red Storm** climbed all the way from the middle to the penthouse, jumping two spots to No. 1 at 2-0 behind their W2 streak. The **Huskies** and **Bulldogs** each slid a rung to second and third respectively — both now 1-1 and both riding an L1 — the Huskies undone by their no-show, the Bulldogs by the narrowest of margins. The forfeit wins nudged the **Knicks** and **Green Wave** up a spot apiece, while **Black Mamba** dropped two to the cellar at 0-2 on an L2 skid.

## Movers & Shakers

**Star of the Week: David Orr (Red Storm).** When your line reads 25 points, 12 rebounds, 2 assists, a steal and 2 blocks, and you win by two, you were the difference. Orr led the league in scoring *and* rebounding (12) *and* tied for the block lead (2). MVP night.

**Sharpshooter of the Week: Khalid Jabarkhail (Bulldogs).** Six made threes on the way to 20 points. On a night the Bulldogs came up two short, Khalid gave them every chance — and a highlight reel to boot.

**The Double-Double Club** welcomed two members, both in Red Storm colors. Orr headlined it, and Erxleben joined with his 12-and-10, adding the muscle that a two-point win demands.

**Category kings:** Orr swept boards and blocks, while **Mason Carey (Bulldogs)** paced the league in assists with 3, sharing the floor generously in his blue-collar effort. And a tip of the cap to **Danny Gardner (Red Storm)**, whose 4 steals led all comers — a reminder that Red Storm's defense had a lot to do with sitting atop this table.
