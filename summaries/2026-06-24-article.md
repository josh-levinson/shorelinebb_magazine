# Red Storm Weather the Storm, Steal a Two-Point Thriller

A one-possession classic at East Shoreline Catholic Academy sent the Red Storm to the top of the table, while two forfeit-line results reshuffled the standings behind them.

## Around the League

The marquee matchup of the week gave us everything: **Red Storm 46, Bulldogs 44**, decided by a single bucket. The Bulldogs got a scorching night from **Khalid Jabarkhail**, who splashed **six threes** on his way to a **20-point** line that also included 6 rebounds, 2 assists, a steal and a block. On most nights, that's a headline-grabbing performance good enough to win the game. Not this one.

Instead, the Red Storm leaned on **David Orr**, who turned in the best all-around outing of the week — **25 points, 12 rebounds, 2 assists, a steal and 2 blocks**, a two-way masterclass that anchored both ends. He got serious help from **Jason Erxleben**, who quietly stuffed the sheet for **12 points, 10 rebounds**, 2 assists, a steal and a block. The Bulldogs did what they could to keep pace — **Joe Flaherty** chipped in 8 points, while **Dan Scandalito** (5 points, 6 boards, 3 assists) and **Mason Carey** (4 points, 7 rebounds, 3 assists, 2 blocks) filled the margins — but the Red Storm's twin double-doubles proved to be the difference in a 2-point escape.

The night's other two results went final on the scoresheet without the fireworks. The **Knicks edged the Huskies 2-0**, and the **Green Wave slipped past Black Mamba 2-0** — a pair of tight, low-event decisions that still count all the same in the win column. Two points, two wins, no complaints.

## The Standings

The story at the top is all Red Storm. Sitting at **2-0** with a **W2** streak, they climbed two spots to claim first place after their gritty win over the Bulldogs. Both the Huskies and Bulldogs took a step back, sliding one rung apiece to second and third after picking up their first losses of the young season. The Bulldogs, despite a slim 88-87 aggregate points margin, land at 1-1 following their heartbreaker.

The night's biggest beneficiaries were the **Knicks** and **Green Wave**, each moving up a spot on the back of their wins. At the bottom, Black Mamba drops two places to sixth, still searching for their first tally at 0-2.

## Movers & Shakers

**Star of the Week — David Orr, Red Storm.** There's no debating it: a **25-8-12** brand of dominance is hard to argue with, and Orr paced the league in both **rebounds (12)** and **blocks (2)** while leading all scorers. When your best player fills every column, good things follow.

**Sharpshooter of the Week — Khalid Jabarkhail, Bulldogs.** Six made threes! Jabarkhail's **20-point** night from deep was the week's best perimeter display, and he did it while grabbing 6 boards and swiping a steal. In a two-point loss, he was a made shot or two away from being the hero.

**Double-Double Club.** Two names punched their tickets this week, and they wore the same jersey: **David Orr** (25 & 12) and **Jason Erxleben** (12 & 10). The Red Storm's frontcourt tandem doubling up on the same night was, unsurprisingly, the recipe for first place.

**Category Kings.** **Mason Carey** topped the league in assists with **3 dimes** to go with his 7 boards and 2 blocks — a do-everything line for the Bulldogs. And on the defensive end, **Danny Gardner** of the Red Storm led all comers with **4 steals**, a pesky reminder that Red Storm's win was built on more than just the box-score headliners.

See you next Wednesday at East Shoreline — the Red Storm have a target on their backs now.
