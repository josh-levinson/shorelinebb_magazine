# McDonald Stuffs the Stat Sheet, Red Storm Stay Perfect on Hump-Day Hoops

A 33-and-23 masterpiece, an 18-point statement from the league's only unbeaten, and a Knicks comeback in the East Shoreline Catholic Academy gym — the first Wednesday of July delivered fireworks a few days early.

## Around the League

**Red Storm 64, Huskies 46.** The league's lone unbeaten kept the zero in the loss column, and they did it with a balanced attack that the Huskies simply couldn't match. **David Orr** was everywhere, stuffing the sheet with 16 points, 15 rebounds, three assists, a block and a pair of triples. **Jason Erxleben** splashed three treys on his way to 14, and **Danny Gardner** flirted with a triple-double, posting 12 points, 10 boards and a game-high six dimes. The problem for the Huskies? **Ryan Florentine** was magnificent — 29 points, 16 rebounds, five steals and two blocks — and it still wasn't nearly enough, as Red Storm pulled away by 18. **Jon Fiorillo** chipped in 11 with a couple of threes, but the supporting cast couldn't keep pace.

**Knicks 53, Black Mamba 47.** Black Mamba hosted, but the Knicks left with the win behind a big night from **Taylor Houghton**, who filled it up for 26 points to go with nine rebounds, five assists, four steals and two blocks. **Jeremy Staub** was a menace on both ends — 13 points, three treys, four steals — and **Eric Hogarth** did the dirty work for a 12-point, 11-rebound double-double. **Colby Morawski** did his best to keep the hosts afloat with 17 points and eight boards, and **Paul Votto** added a nine-point, 10-rebound effort, but the Mamba came up six short and remain in search of that first win.

**Green Wave 61, Bulldogs 40.** This one belonged to **Jeremy McDonald**, and it wasn't close. His 33-point, 23-rebound, five-assist, five-steal line was the stat sheet of the week, the kind of one-man wrecking crew performance that turns a game into a formality. **Tyler Copple** provided plenty of thunder himself with 20 points and two blocks, giving Green Wave a devastating one-two punch. **Khalid Jabarkhail** led the Bulldogs with 14 and a pair of threes, and **Mason Carey** nearly notched a double-double with 10 and nine, but Green Wave cruised by 21.

## The Standings

**Red Storm** sit alone at the top at a perfect 3-0, riding a plus-23 differential and a W3 streak. The big mover of the week is **Green Wave**, who leapt three spots into second at 2-1 on the strength of their blowout. The **Knicks** climbed a rung to third, also at 2-1, thanks to their road win. It was a rougher night below the cut line: both the **Huskies** and **Bulldogs** slid two spots after taking their second straight losses, while **Black Mamba** hold steady at the bottom, still hunting for win No. 1.

## Movers & Shakers

**Star of the week** goes to **Jeremy McDonald**, whose 33 points and 23 rebounds led the league in both scoring and boards — and he tacked on five assists and five steals for good measure. Absurd.

Right behind him, **Ryan Florentine** was a two-way terror in a losing effort, dropping 29 points with 16 rebounds and pacing the entire league in both steals (5) and blocks (2). **Taylor Houghton** rounded out the 20-point club with his 26-piece for the victorious Knicks.

The **dimes crown** belongs to **Danny Gardner**, whose six assists topped the week as part of his tidy double-double.

Speaking of which, it was a big night for **double-doubles** — five in all: Gardner and **David Orr** for Red Storm, Florentine for the Huskies, **Eric Hogarth** for the Knicks, and, of course, McDonald's monster line for Green Wave.
