# Girolamo's 33 Power Green Wave to a One-Point Survival at East Shoreline

A 33-point eruption, a buzzer-tight finish, and a pair of Huskies double-doubles headlined a loaded Wednesday night on the hardwood — proof that midsummer basketball at East Shoreline Catholic Academy is heating up right along with the weather.

## Around the League

**Huskies 49, Black Mamba 36.** The Huskies leaned on their frontcourt tandem to grind out a 13-point win, and both halves of that duo posted double-doubles. Ryan Florentine did a little of everything — 23 points, 12 rebounds, two assists, two steals, two blocks and four made threes — while Neil Anglemire turned the glass into his personal property, hauling in a monstrous 21 rebounds to go with 13 points and four assists. Black Mamba got a team-high 11 from Colby Morawski (six boards) and balanced contributions from Samim Jabarkhail (8 points, 5 rebounds) and Paul Votto (8 points, 7 rebounds, 3 assists), but couldn't solve the Huskies' size inside.

**Green Wave 52, Red Storm 51.** The game of the week, no contest. Jimmy Girolamo put the Green Wave on his back with a spectacular 33-point, seven-rebound, four-steal night, drilling three from deep and single-handedly matching Red Storm's best punches. And Red Storm punched hard: David Orr answered with 24 points and a scorching five made threes, adding seven rebounds, two steals and two blocks. Danny Gardner flirted with a double-double (10 points, 9 rebounds, 3 assists) and Doug Brackney and James Holt each chipped in stat-sheet-stuffing lines. In the end, Tyler Copple's 11-point, four-assist effort and Patrick Boutilier's five dimes were just enough to nudge the Wave across the line by a single point.

**Knicks 60, Bulldogs 53.** Khalid Jabarkhail was must-watch television, pouring in 30 points on four made threes with five boards and two steals — but the Knicks had one more gear. Even a supporting cast that included Mason Carey's 11-point, eight-rebound double-double effort and Joe Flaherty's pair of triples couldn't get the Bulldogs over the top, as the Knicks rolled to a 60-53 win and their third straight victory.

## The Standings

Log-jam alert at the top: three teams sit at 3-1. **Red Storm** cling to first on the strength of a plus-22 point differential, though that heartbreaking one-point loss to Green Wave snapped their momentum and dropped them to an L1 skid. Right on their heels, **Green Wave** (W3) and the **Knicks** (W3) are surging, both riding three-game win streaks and daring the leaders to look over their shoulders. The **Huskies** got well at 2-2 with a comfortable win, while the **Bulldogs** (1-3, L3) and winless **Black Mamba** (0-4, L4) are still searching for traction. No one changed rank this week, but the pecking order feels one Wednesday away from a shakeup.

## Movers & Shakers

**Scoring champ:** Take a bow, **Jimmy Girolamo** (Green Wave), whose 33-point, four-steal masterpiece was the week's high-water mark — and the difference in a one-point win. **Khalid Jabarkhail** (Bulldogs) wasn't far behind with 30, and **David Orr** (Red Storm) lit it up for 24 on a league-best five made threes.

**Glass cleaner:** **Neil Anglemire** (Huskies) grabbed a jaw-dropping 21 rebounds, comfortably the top mark of the week.

**Category kings:** **Patrick Boutilier** (Green Wave) dished a week-high five assists, Girolamo's four steals paced the league in takeaways, and **Ryan Florentine** (Huskies) topped the block column with two.

**Double-double club:** Both belong to the Huskies. Florentine went for 23 & 12, and Anglemire posted 13 & 21 — a two-man wrecking crew that turned Wednesday into a clinic on interior dominance.
