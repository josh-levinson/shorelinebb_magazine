# McDonald Drops 39 as Green Wave Ride the Crest to the Top

The Green Wave surfed to their fourth straight win behind a monster night from Jeremy McDonald, while Black Mamba snapped a losing skid by three and the Huskies quietly climbed the ladder. It was a Wednesday at East Shoreline Catholic Academy where the buckets came in bunches.

## Around the League

**Green Wave 59, Knicks 36.** When Jeremy McDonald gets going, there's not much you can draw up to stop it. The Green Wave star authored a near-complete stat sheet — 39 points, 13 rebounds, 3 assists, 5 steals and a pair of threes — in a 23-point demolition of the Knicks. He had help, too: Shawn Love splashed five triples on his way to 15 points, and Tyler Copple did the dirty work with 11 boards and a game-high 5 assists. The Knicks had no answer despite Alex Hetherington's 17 points (7 rebounds, 3 threes) and 12 from Brian Jackson. Scott Edmond's three blocks were a bright spot in a tough night on the scoreboard.

**Huskies 51, Bulldogs 43.** Ryan Florentine was the best player on the floor not named McDonald this week, torching the Bulldogs for 28 points on four made threes, adding 3 steals for good measure. But the box-score curiosity of the night belonged to teammate Neil Anglemire, who hauled in a staggering **28 rebounds** to go with 5 points and 4 assists — a glass-cleaning masterclass. Scott Houghton chipped in 9. The Bulldogs got a sharpshooting 19 from Dan Scandalito (five threes) and a tidy 11-and-7 from Khalid Jabarkhail, but it wasn't enough to keep pace as the Huskies pulled away for an eight-point win.

**Black Mamba 53, Red Storm 50.** The night's nail-biter went down to the wire. Colby Morawski powered Black Mamba with a 19-point, 11-rebound double-double, Paul Votto orchestrated with 14 points and 4 assists, and Mike Iannuzzi (9 points, three threes) provided the outside spacing. Red Storm made them earn it — Jason Erxleben poured in 21 with 9 boards and four triples, Branden LaPia stuffed the sheet with 12 points, 4 assists and 2 steals, and Colin Ryan added 11 and 7. In the end, a three-point margin was the difference, and Black Mamba got to exhale.

## The Standings

The top of the table has a new occupant: the **Green Wave** climb to No. 1 on the strength of their four-game winning streak and a league-best +38 point differential. Their rise nudged the **Red Storm** down to second, where a second straight loss has cooled a team that still boasts the most points scored in the league (254). The **Huskies** are surging, up a spot to third on the back of two straight wins, while the **Knicks** slip to fourth. At the bottom, **Black Mamba** finally got in the win column and climbed a rung, sending the **Bulldogs** to the cellar amid a four-game slide despite plenty of firepower on offense.

## Movers & Shakers

**Star of the Week: Jeremy McDonald (Green Wave).** Thirty-nine points. Thirteen rebounds. Five steals. He led the entire league in scoring *and* steals in the same night while grabbing a double-double. Case closed.

**The Scoring Leaderboard.** Behind McDonald came Ryan Florentine's 28 for the Huskies, Jason Erxleben's 21 for Red Storm, and a tie at 19 between Dan Scandalito (five threes!) and Colby Morawski.

**Category Kings.** Neil Anglemire's **28 rebounds** is the number everyone will be talking about — a jaw-dropper on the boards. Tyler Copple paced all playmakers with 5 assists, McDonald tied for the steals crown with 5, and Scott Edmond swatted a league-high 3 blocks.

**Double-Double Club.** Two names punched their tickets: McDonald (39/13) and Morawski (19/11), the latter carrying Black Mamba to their first win.
