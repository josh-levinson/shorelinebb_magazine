# Huskies Ride the Hot Hand as Black Mamba Snap Back to Life

A midweek slate at East Shoreline Catholic Academy delivered a big road statement, a five-point shootout, and one of the more unusual final scores you'll ever see. Buckle up.

## Around the League

The night's marquee result belonged to the **Huskies**, who marched into Green Wave's building and left with a **57–47** victory that felt every bit as complete as the box score suggests. Ryan Florentine did a little bit of everything, stuffing the sheet with 21 points, 10 rebounds, 3 assists, a steal and a triple, while Josh Carter matched him blow for blow inside — 16 points, 12 boards, 3 dimes and a pair of threes. Toss in Jon Fiorillo's tidy 6-point, 5-assist, 5-rebound line and the Huskies simply had too many hands in the pot. For the hosts, Shawn Love was a one-man wrecking crew with a game-high 26 points to go with 6 rebounds, 4 assists and 2 steals, but Green Wave couldn't find enough support. Jeffrey Luman controlled the glass with a monstrous 15 rebounds (plus 7 points), and Patrick Boutilier chipped in 8 points on two threes, yet 47 wasn't enough to keep pace.

In the night's tightest contest, **Black Mamba** held off the **Bulldogs 52–47** in a game that came down to shot-making. Colby Morawski was electric with 25 points, and he got timely help from Mike Iannuzzi (9 points, 6 rebounds) and Samim Jabarkhail, whose 7 points, 9 rebounds and 3 assists helped Mamba survive a scorching night from the visitors. Because Dan Scandalito was *feeling it* — 22 points on five made threes, plus 6 boards and 3 assists — and Khalid Jabarkhail did the dirty work with 13 points, 7 rebounds and 2 blocks. The Bulldogs got contributions across the board from Mason Carey (8 points, 8 rebounds) and Dimitrios Varrias, but the five-point gap held.

And then there was the box score that will launch a hundred group-chat theories: the **Red Storm** edged the **Knicks 2–0**. With no individual lines to report, we'll simply tip our cap to whoever put in that lone bucket and log a win in the standings that counts just the same as any other.

## The Standings

It's tight at the top. The **Green Wave** cling to first at 4–2 despite the loss, their +28 point differential still the best in the league. But the logjam right behind them is real: the **Red Storm** (4–2, riding a W1) and the surging **Huskies** (4–2, now winners of three straight) are breathing down their necks, separated only by the fine print. The **Knicks** slipped to 3–3 and a two-game skid, while **Black Mamba** are quietly heating up — that win makes it two in a row and nudges them back toward the pack at 2–4. The **Bulldogs** remain in search of a spark, sitting at 1–5 on a five-game slide, though their 271 points scored is proof the offense isn't the problem.

## Movers & Shakers

**Bucket of the Week** goes to Green Wave's **Shawn Love**, whose 26-point night led all scorers and came with 6 rebounds, 4 assists and a league-best 2 steals for good measure. Right on his heels: **Colby Morawski** poured in 25 in Black Mamba's win, and **Dan Scandalito** dropped 22 while drilling a night-high five three-pointers.

The **double-double club** was an all-Huskies affair. **Ryan Florentine** (21 & 10) and **Josh Carter** (16 & 12) both cracked double figures in points and boards, powering that road win — a fitting reward for the team playing the best ball in the league right now.

On the category leaderboard, **Jeffrey Luman** cleaned the glass like nobody else with 15 rebounds, **Jon Fiorillo** dished a week-high 5 assists, and **Khalid Jabarkhail** anchored the paint with 2 blocks. Different roles, same result: winning plays.
