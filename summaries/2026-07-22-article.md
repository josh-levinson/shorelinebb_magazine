# Huskies Ride Their Wave, Mamba Strikes Back at Shoreline

The East Shoreline Catholic Academy hardwood delivered a little bit of everything on Wednesday night: a road ambush, a shootout down to the wire, and a no-show that never got off the ground.

## Around the League

**Huskies 57, Green Wave 47.** The league's top team hosted, but the Huskies never treated the Green Wave like the No. 1 seed. **Ryan Florentine** set the tone with a 21-point, 10-rebound double-double, chipping in 3 assists, a steal and a three, while **Josh Carter** matched him blow for blow with 16 points and a game-high-tying 12 boards to go with 3 assists and a pair of triples. Behind that frontcourt muscle, the Huskies pulled away for a 10-point win, with **Jon Fiorillo** quarterbacking the offense (6 points, 5 rebounds, 5 assists) and **John DeMetro** adding 7. Green Wave's **Shawn Love** did just about everything he could — a game-high 26 points with 6 rebounds, 4 assists and 2 steals — but the supporting cast couldn't keep pace. **Jeffrey Luman** was a monster on the glass with 15 rebounds and 7 points, and **Patrick Boutilier** knocked down two threes in an 8-point night, though it wasn't enough to stop the Huskies' surge.

**Black Mamba 52, Bulldogs 47.** In the night's tightest true contest, Black Mamba leaned on a big scoring effort from **Colby Morawski**, who poured in 25 points with 5 rebounds. The Bulldogs had an answer in **Dan Scandalito**, who caught fire from deep — 5 threes on his way to 22 points, with 6 rebounds, 3 assists and a steal — but the rest of the ledger tilted Black Mamba's way. **Mike Iannuzzi** (9 points, 6 rebounds), **Samim Jabarkhail** (7 points, 9 rebounds, 3 assists) and **Paul Votto** (6 points, 5 rebounds, a block) gave the hosts the balance they needed to survive by five. **Khalid Jabarkhail** did his part in the loss with 13 points, 7 rebounds and 2 blocks, and **Mason Carey** added 8 and 8, but the Bulldogs came up just short.

**Red Storm 2, Knicks 0 (forfeit).** No box score here — the Knicks couldn't field a team, so Red Storm takes the win by the league's standard 2-0 forfeit convention. A quiet night on the scoreboard, but a valuable notch in the standings all the same.

## The Standings

It's tight at the top, where three teams sit at 4-2 and are separated only by point differential. **Green Wave** clings to first despite the loss, its +28 diff still the best in the league, but the Huskies are lurking — winners of three straight and climbing into the conversation. **Red Storm** holds second on the strength of the forfeit win. The **Knicks** slip to a 3-3 split and an L2 skid, while **Black Mamba** is quietly building momentum with two straight wins to move to 2-4. The **Bulldogs**, meanwhile, remain winless-adjacent at 1-5 and riding a five-game slide — though with 271 points scored, the offense clearly isn't the problem.

## Movers & Shakers

**Bucket of the week** belongs to **Shawn Love**, whose 26-point, 6-rebound, 4-assist, 2-steal line paced all scorers even in a losing effort. Right on his heels was **Colby Morawski** with 25 in a winning cause, and **Dan Scandalito**, whose 5 made threes lit up the gym for 22.

The **double-double club** was a Huskies-only affair: **Ryan Florentine** (21 & 10) and **Josh Carter** (16 & 12) both cleared the bar in the same game — a big reason the Huskies are on a three-game roll.

Around the categories: **Jeffrey Luman** cleaned the glass better than anyone with **15 rebounds**, **Jon Fiorillo** dished a week-best **5 assists**, and **Khalid Jabarkhail** anchored the paint with **2 blocks**. On the steal front, **Shawn Love** topped the leaderboard with **2**.
