# Mamba Strikes, Knicks Survive, and the Green Wave Keeps Rolling

A Wednesday night at East Shoreline Catholic Academy delivered a 31-point eruption, a seven-steal takeover, and a two-point thriller — proof that this league still has plenty to sort out down the stretch.

## Around the League

**Black Mamba 63, Huskies 51.** Ryan Florentine did everything humanly possible to keep the Huskies afloat, pouring in a game-high **31 points** with 9 rebounds, 2 blocks and 4 threes — the kind of night that usually wins ballgames. This one didn't, because Black Mamba answered with a balanced barrage. **Colby Morawski** led the way with 20 points, 8 boards and 3 assists, **Mike Iannuzzi** splashed 4 threes on his way to 12, and **Samim Jabarkhail** stuffed the sheet with a 10-point, 11-rebound, 5-assist double-double. Don't overlook **Dan Greif**, either — 11 points, 8 rebounds and a game-swinging 5 steals. Scott Houghton chipped in 16 for the Huskies, but 12 was the final margin.

**Green Wave 47, Red Storm 37.** In a defensive rock fight, **Jimmy Giorlamo** turned the game into his personal highlight reel: **21 points, 7 steals, 2 blocks and 3 threes**. When you're single-handedly generating that many turnovers, the scoreboard tends to follow. **Jeremy McDonald** backed him up with a 13-point, 10-rebound double-double, adding 4 steals of his own. Red Storm's **David Orr** was terrific in a losing effort — 19 points and 10 boards — but got too little help around him, as Branden LaPia's 7 was the next-best line. Green Wave 47, Red Storm 37.

**Knicks 44, Bulldogs 42.** The nightcap was the nail-biter. The Knicks held on by a bucket behind **Taylor Houghton's** double-double (13 points, 10 rebounds) and 12 from **Alex Hetherington**, who hit a pair of threes. **Brian Jackson** was a menace on the perimeter with 5 steals to go with 6 points and 7 boards. The Bulldogs made them sweat: **Anthoni Reyes** dropped 16 with 3 triples and 4 steals, and **Dimitrios Varrias** added a well-rounded 13 points, 8 rebounds and 4 assists. Two points was all that separated them — and for the Bulldogs, close games are becoming a painful theme.

## The Standings

The **Green Wave** remain kings of the hill, extending to a W2 streak and a league-best +58 point differential atop the table. The **Huskies** hold second despite Florentine's brilliance going for naught, snapping into an L1 after the loss to Black Mamba. The night's biggest riser is the **Knicks**, whose two-point win pushed them up a spot into third and stretched their streak to W2. That climb came at the expense of **Red Storm**, who slid to fourth on an L2 skid. **Black Mamba** snapped a rough patch with a feel-good W1, while the **Bulldogs**, despite a spirited near-miss, remain winless since way back — that heartbreaker dropped them to L7.

## Movers & Shakers

**Bucket of the week:** **Ryan Florentine** takes it in a walk. His 31-point, 9-rebound, 4-three showing was the highest scoring line on the floor by a full ten points — a one-man show, even in defeat.

**Two-way terror:** **Jimmy Giorlamo** paired 21 points with a league-leading **7 steals** and 2 blocks. Nobody bent a game to their will quite like him this week.

**The scoring board** rounded out with **Colby Morawski** (20), **David Orr** (19), and a 16-point tie between **Scott Houghton** and **Anthoni Reyes**.

**Category kings:** **Paul Votto** cleaned the glass with a week-high **11 rebounds** for Black Mamba, **Samim Jabarkhail** dished a week-best **5 assists**, and **Sean Perkins** anchored the paint with **4 blocks** for the Huskies.

**Double-double club:** four members this week — **Jabarkhail** (10 & 11), **Orr** (19 & 10), **McDonald** (13 & 10), and **Taylor Houghton** (13 & 10). Do-it-all nights, every one.
