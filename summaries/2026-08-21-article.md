# Red Storm Own the Glass, Green Wave Make It Three, and Anglemire Nearly Does It All

Wednesday at East Shoreline Catholic Academy gave us the week's only blowout, a 16-rebound night from a big man who refused to be moved, and a Huskies forward who finished three assists short of a triple-double.

## Around the League

**Red Storm 78, Black Mamba 62.** This was the week's loudest scoreboard, and Red Storm earned every point of it on the boards. **David Orr** was immovable: **23 points and 17 rebounds** with 3 assists and 2 steals, both of them week-high marks in scoring and rebounding. **Branden LaPia** ran the show alongside him with 21 points, 9 rebounds and a week-best **7 assists**, knocking down 4 threes in the process. **Colin Ryan** quietly added a 15-point, 14-rebound double-double, and **James Holt** came off with 11 on 3 triples. Black Mamba did not go quietly — **Paul Votto** posted 22 points and 11 rebounds, **Samim Jabarkhail** answered with 19 and 13 plus 4 steals and 4 threes, and **Mike Iannuzzi** was the week's most prolific shooter from deep, hitting **5 threes** on his way to 17. Three double-digit scorers, and it still wasn't enough against a Red Storm team that simply owned the second chances. **Josh Levinson** grabbed 7 boards in the effort.

**Huskies 55, Bulldogs 45.** The line of the night belonged to **Neil Anglemire**, who put up **15 points, 16 rebounds and 7 assists** with 3 steals — three assists away from a triple-double and the most complete stat sheet anyone filled out all week. **Ryan Florentine** led the Huskies in scoring with 17 points, 7 rebounds and 3 steals, and **Scott Houghton** added 11. The Bulldogs spread it around — **Joe Flaherty** paced them with 12, **Mason Carey** went for 10 and 7, **Anthoni Reyes** chipped in 9 points and 5 assists on a pair of threes, and **Blake Walker** filled in 6 points, 7 rebounds and 4 assists — but they've now dropped eight straight, and no team in the league is working harder for less return.

**Green Wave 54, Knicks 44.** The league leaders made it three in a row, and they did it the way they usually do: with **Jeremy McDonald** everywhere at once. He finished with **21 points, 14 rebounds, 5 steals and 2 blocks**, leading the week in both steals and blocks. **Jimmy Giorlamo** was the perfect complement — 19 points on 4 threes, with 5 rebounds, 5 assists, 2 steals and a block. **Shawn Love** added 8. The Knicks got a strong all-around game from **Brian Jackson** (14 points, 8 rebounds, 5 assists) and a tidy 10-and-10 from **Scott Edmond**, with 9 from **Jeremy Staub** and 7 from **Alex Hetherington** — but they couldn't close the ten-point gap, and the loss cost them a spot in the table.

## The Standings

The **Green Wave** stay on top and keep pulling away, now 7-2 with a W3 streak and a league-best +68 differential. The **Huskies** sit second at 6-3 after taking care of business. The week's mover is **Red Storm**, whose 16-point win lifted them a spot into third at 5-4 — they and the **Knicks** share identical records, but Red Storm's +10 differential edges out the Knicks' -9, and that's the whole story of the swap. **Black Mamba** slipped to 3-6, and the **Bulldogs** remain 1-8 on an L8 skid, still searching for their second win.

## Movers & Shakers

**Line of the week:** **Neil Anglemire**, 15 points, 16 rebounds, 7 assists, 3 steals. Nobody else came near that spread.

**The scoring leaderboard:** **David Orr** (23) edged **Paul Votto** (22), with **Branden LaPia** and **Jeremy McDonald** tied at 21 and **Samim Jabarkhail** and **Jimmy Giorlamo** tied at 19.

**Category kings:** Orr took the glass with **17 rebounds**, LaPia led the passing with **7 assists**, and McDonald swept the defensive categories with **5 steals and 2 blocks**. From distance, **Mike Iannuzzi's 5 threes** topped the week, with Giorlamo, LaPia and Jabarkhail each burying 4.

**Double-double club:** seven strong this week — **Orr** (23 & 17), **Votto** (22 & 11), **McDonald** (21 & 14), **Jabarkhail** (19 & 13), **Anglemire** (15 & 16), **Ryan** (15 & 14) and **Edmond** (10 & 10).
