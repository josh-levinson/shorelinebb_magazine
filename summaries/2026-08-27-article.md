# McDonald Fills Every Column as Green Wave Hold Serve at the Top

The Shoreline men's league packed a stat-sheet stuffer, a three-point shootout, and a couple of nail-biters into one Wednesday night at East Shoreline Catholic Academy — and the top of the table didn't budge.

## Around the League

The headline act was **Jeremy McDonald**, who turned in the kind of line that makes a scorekeeper's hand cramp. In the **Green Wave's 63-59** win over **Black Mamba**, McDonald poured in 38 points and grabbed 17 rebounds, then added 6 assists, 5 steals and 3 blocks for good measure — a near quadruple-flirtation that touched every category on the sheet. He got help where it mattered: **Patrick Boutilier** knocked down three triples on his way to 14 points, and **Tyler Copple** anchored the paint with 8 points, 17 rebounds and 4 blocks. Black Mamba had every reason to stick around — **Colby Morawski** was terrific with 23 points and 10 boards, and **Paul Votto** chipped in 16 points, 9 rebounds and 5 assists — but four points was as close as they'd get.

Over in the night's grittiest tilt, the **Huskies survived 50-47** against the **Knicks** in a game that came down to the final possessions. **Ryan Florentine** carried the scoring load with 24 points and 11 boards, drilling three from deep, while **Neil Anglemire** did just about everything else — a monstrous 23 rebounds to go with 8 assists and 3 steals. The Knicks got 15 from **Jeremy Staub** on three triples and a well-rounded 8-point, 12-rebound, 5-assist night from **Brian Jackson**, but couldn't quite dig out of a three-point hole.

The **Red Storm** handled the **Bulldogs 64-54** behind a pair of scoring guards: **Branden LaPia** with 22 points and **Jason Erxleben** with 21 points, 4 steals and a nose for the ball. **David Orr** quietly stacked a near double-double of 9 points, 12 rebounds and 6 assists. The Bulldogs got a valiant fight from **Mason Carey**, whose 21 points, 17 rebounds and four made threes were the best any Bulldog has looked in a while — but the supporting cast topped out at nine points, and Red Storm pulled away for the 10-point win.

## The Standings

No reshuffling this week — every team held its position, but the storylines underneath are shifting. **Green Wave** stay perched at No. 1, stretching their win streak to four and pushing their point differential to a league-best +72. The **Huskies** kept pace at second with their second straight win, while **Red Storm** made it two in a row of their own to hold third. The **Knicks** slipped to a two-game skid but cling to fourth, and **Black Mamba** dropped a close one to fall to 3-7. The **Bulldogs**, despite Carey's heroics, remain winless on the season at 1-9, their skid now at nine.

## Movers & Shakers

- **Star of the week:** No debate. **Jeremy McDonald** (Green Wave) led all scorers with 38 points and topped the league in steals with 5, all while flirting with a triple-double thanks to 17 boards, 6 assists and 3 blocks.
- **Scoring chase:** Behind McDonald came the Huskies' **Ryan Florentine** (24), Black Mamba's **Colby Morawski** (23), and a Red Storm duo of **Branden LaPia** (22) and **Jason Erxleben** (21) — with Bulldogs' **Mason Carey** matching that 21.
- **Glass and dimes:** **Neil Anglemire** (Huskies) swept two categories at once, leading the week with 23 rebounds *and* 8 assists.
- **Rim protection:** **Tyler Copple** (Green Wave) paced everyone with 4 blocks.
- **Double-doubles:** Four of them this week — McDonald, Florentine, Morawski and Carey all posted double-digit points and rebounds.
