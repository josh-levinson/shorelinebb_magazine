# McDonald's 38 Powers Green Wave Past Black Mamba in a Wednesday Worth the Trip

The Green Wave keep rolling, the Huskies keep winning close, and one man in a green jersey did just about everything you can do on a basketball floor. Here's how Wednesday night at East Shoreline Catholic Academy shook out.

## Around the League

**Green Wave 63, Black Mamba 59.** If you needed one line to explain why the Green Wave sit atop the table, Jeremy McDonald offered it: 38 points, 17 rebounds, 6 assists, 5 steals and 3 blocks, filling every column on the sheet and dragging his team to a four-point win. He wasn't alone — Patrick Boutilier knocked down three triples for 14, and Tyler Copple did the quiet dirty work with 8 points, 17 boards and 4 blocks of his own. Black Mamba pushed right to the wire behind Colby Morawski's 23 and 10 and a tidy 16-point, 9-rebound, 5-assist night from Paul Votto, but couldn't quite reel the Wave back in. That's a fourth straight win for the league leaders.

**Huskies 50, Knicks 47.** A grinder, and the Huskies had just enough. Ryan Florentine led the way with 24 points and 11 rebounds on three made threes, while Neil Anglemire turned in one of the more remarkable stat lines you'll see in any league: 23 rebounds and 8 assists to go with 7 points, 3 steals and a block. The Knicks got 15 and three triples from Jeremy Staub and a well-rounded 8-point, 12-rebound, 5-assist showing from Brian Jackson, but the three-point margin held up. The loss drops the Knicks into a two-game slide.

**Red Storm 64, Bulldogs 54.** Branden LaPia (22 points, three assists, two threes) and Jason Erxleben (21 points, four steals) gave the Red Storm a backcourt punch the Bulldogs couldn't match. David Orr stuffed the sheet with 9 points, 12 rebounds and 6 assists to round out the effort. Mason Carey did everything he could for the Bulldogs — 21 points, 17 rebounds and four made threes — but the supporting cast topped out at nine, and the Red Storm pulled away for a 10-point win and a second straight victory.

## The Standings

No reshuffling at the top this week — every team held its position — but the story is momentum. The **Green Wave** (8-2) sit comfortably in first on a four-game heater and a league-best +72 point differential. The **Huskies** (7-3) keep pace at second with their second straight win, while the **Red Storm** (6-4) have quietly won two in a row to hold third. The **Knicks** (5-5) slipped into a two-game skid and are treading water at .500, with the **Black Mamba** (3-7) also losing their last two. And the **Bulldogs** (1-9) remain in a tough spot, their losing streak now stretched to nine despite Carey's best efforts.

## Movers & Shakers

**The headliner:** Jeremy McDonald doesn't need much introduction after a 38-point night, but the rest of the line is where the jaw drops — 17 rebounds, 6 assists, 5 steals (tops in the league this week) and 3 blocks. A complete afternoon of work.

**Scoring board:** Behind McDonald came Florentine (24), Morawski (23), and a trio at 21-22: LaPia's 22 for the Red Storm, plus Erxleben and Carey each at 21.

**Glass and dishes:** Neil Anglemire owned the boards with a staggering 23 rebounds and led all passers with 8 assists — a double-double of a very unusual flavor. Tyler Copple paced the league with 4 blocks.

**Double-double club:** Four members this week — McDonald, Florentine, Morawski, and Carey, whose 21-and-17 with four threes deserved a better team result.
